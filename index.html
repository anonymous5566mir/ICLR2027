ProCraft: Instruction-Driven Music Production from Free-Form Text

Audio examples for the paper. ProCraft is a 0.5B codec language model that edits a 30-second mix from a freely phrased instruction: removing, extracting, accompanying, covering, re-instrumenting, denoising, and applying audio effects.

All systems run on the same test items with their released weights. Clips are loudness-normalised mono, 30 seconds.

ProCraft (ours) ★ deterministic signal processing or separation Slakh: synthetic multitracks · MoisesDB: real recordings Click a row to play; click its bar to seek. One clip plays at a time.

Training pairs

Pairs drawn from the on-the-fly training pipeline, one per conditioning case and domain: the instruction, the executed audio effect calls, and the source and target audio.

edit + audio effectsSlakh

“Remove the organ. Add cavernous hall wash to the bass, vinyl crackle bed to the drums, crushed 8-bit grit to the guitar, echo delay shimmer to the piano, and ambient noise haze to the ensemble.”

remove_track organ
apply_fx bass sox_reverb reverberance=50 high_freq_damping=50 room_scale=50 stereo_depth=50 pre_delay=20 wet_gain=-3
apply_fx drums am_add_color_noise snr_db=10 f_decay=2
apply_fx guitar am_bit_crush bit_depth=9
apply_fx piano sox_echo gain_in=0.7 gain_out=0.6 delay_ms=120 decay=0.4
apply_fx ensemble am_add_gaussian_noise amplitude=0.008
